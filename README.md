## Overview

PIUM (Phase Interference Universe Model) is an ontological interpretive framework in which observable physical phenomena are interpreted as phase and amplitude configurations of a universal wave field Φ(x,t).

The framework does not replace General Relativity, the Standard Model, or standard quantum mechanics.  
It introduces no new particles, forces, or empirical constants.

Instead, PIUM provides a coherent interpretive layer connecting:

- matter as phase-coherent closure,
- gravitation as large-scale phase relaxation,
- galactic dynamics as emergent field organization,
- and quantum entanglement as non-separable phase-correlated structure.

- stable reproducible empirical baselines,
- exploratory morphology programs,
- and broader theoretical interpretation layers.

No claim of complete empirical confirmation is made.
